OUR STORY

  • 2013

    The story begins

    Our first developments were born from the desire to support an adult psychotherapist in her daily practice.

  • 2013 – 2022

    Continuous improvement

    Over ten years, the application was enriched with new features specific to the daily needs of psychologists and psychotherapists.

  • 2023

    Application launch

    With the arrival of psychotherapy reimbursement and repeated requests from colleagues, the application opened to all practitioners under the name Sigmund.

  • 2024

    PID support

    CNS certification and implementation of PID (Immediate Direct Payment) support.

  • 2025

    Application development

    Opening of Sigmund to psychiatrists.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does Sigmund help professionals?
The application reduces the time spent on all administrative activities by bringing several features together in one place, allowing professionals to focus more on their patients.
Where is health data stored?
Health data is stored in the Grand Duchy of Luxembourg and is subject to Luxembourg law as well as the European GDPR, ensuring its security and confidentiality.
What does Sigmund cost?
Sigmund is available for €90/month with no long-term commitment, with responsive support and a range of specific features. With a two-week free trial, there is no reason not to try it.
How do I try Sigmund?
  1. Book a demo by filling in the contact form.
  2. Explore Sigmund during your two-week free trial.
  3. Subscribe for €90/month after your trial.
  4. Our team (Guillaume, Sylvain, Franck) is available to answer your questions.
I only work in practice 2 days a week — is it really useful for me?
Even if you only work 2 days a week, Sigmund can be extremely beneficial by automating administrative tasks, allowing you to focus more on your patients. Many clients work part-time and are delighted to use Sigmund.
Can I import my data from another software?
Yes, our team can help you import your data (patients, sessions, invoices, etc.) from an Excel file or another software.
