Immediate Direct Payment (PID)

Practical guide for mental health professionals in private practice in Luxembourg

Psychiatrist — available now Psychotherapist — coming soon

Approved by the CNS (Caisse Nationale de Santé) in 2024, Immediate Direct Payment is a major step forward for psychiatrists in Luxembourg: the CNS reimburses you directly, so your patients no longer have to pay upfront. Sigmund is the only mental health software PID-approved in the Grand Duchy.

This guide explains how PID works, who can benefit today, and how Sigmund simplifies its day-to-day management.

What is Immediate Direct Payment?

Immediate Direct Payment (PID — Paiement Immédiat Direct) is a CNS scheme that allows the practitioner to be reimbursed directly and immediately by the CNS, without patients having to pay upfront. In practice:

  • The patient no longer has to pay upfront for the CNS-covered share.
  • The practitioner submits the reimbursement request to the CNS via approved software.
  • The CNS pays the practitioner directly at the agreed rate.

Good to know: PID removes the need to pay upfront for the CNS-covered share of the fee. A minor patient therefore pays nothing at the consultation. An adult patient still owes any co-payment not covered by the CNS, to be settled separately with the practitioner.

Who can benefit from PID today?

Psychiatrists — available now

All CNS-contracted psychiatrists can activate PID. The option is now offered free of charge in Sigmund, with no extra cost on top of the base subscription. This is currently the only mental health profession for which PID is operational in Luxembourg.

Psychotherapists — PID is coming soon

The CNS is actively working to extend PID to recognised psychotherapists, following the introduction of psychotherapy reimbursement in 2023. No exact date has been announced yet, but the rollout is underway.

Good news for Sigmund subscribers: as soon as the CNS opens PID to psychotherapists, Sigmund will activate it automatically in your account — free of charge, at no extra cost. No additional steps will be required on your part — you will be among the first to benefit from it.

Psychologists

The PID scheme does not apply to psychologists under the current Luxembourg regulatory framework.

The concrete benefits of PID

For the practitioner

  • Zero CNS bad debts: the CNS pays you directly, eliminating non-payment risk on the reimbursable portion.
  • Better cash flow: reimbursements arrive quickly and predictably.
  • Simplified accounting: fewer reminders to manage, less tracking of individual payments.
  • Competitive advantage: patients prefer practitioners who do not require upfront payment.

For the patient

  • No upfront costs at the consultation.
  • No administrative steps: the practitioner (via Sigmund) handles everything.
  • Easier access to care, particularly for patients on lower incomes.

PID and CNS invoicing: what's the difference?

PID does not replace CNS invoicing — it complements it. In both cases, you issue an invoice in CNS format. The difference is who pays the practitioner:

  • Standard CNS invoicing: the patient pays the practitioner, then claims reimbursement from the CNS. The practitioner manages unpaid invoices and reminders.
  • With PID: the CNS pays the practitioner directly. The patient no longer pays the CNS-covered share upfront — they only settle any remaining co-payment or fee excess above the CNS rate.

Frequently asked questions about PID

Is PID mandatory for contracted psychiatrists?
No, PID is optional. You can continue billing in the standard way (patient pays upfront) and activate PID whenever you choose. Once activated in Sigmund, the system handles everything automatically.
How quickly does the CNS reimburse via PID?
CNS reimbursement timescales via PID are generally shorter than with standard invoicing. Contact our team or the CNS directly for current processing times.
What happens if the CNS rejects a PID request?
If a request is rejected, Sigmund notifies you immediately in your dashboard with the reason for rejection. You can correct and resubmit directly from the application. Our team is available to assist if needed.
Can I activate PID mid-subscription?
Yes, the PID option can be activated at any time from your Sigmund account. It is now offered free of charge, at no extra cost. You can deactivate it at any time.
When will PID be available for psychotherapists?
The rollout of PID for psychotherapists depends on the CNS. No exact date has been announced yet, but the process is actively underway. As soon as the CNS opens it officially, Sigmund will integrate it automatically and free of charge for all psychotherapist subscribers — no additional steps required on their part.
