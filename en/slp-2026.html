What Sigmund does — and doesn't do

What Sigmund handles

  • Your calendar and automatic reminders to your patients
  • Your fee statements, in CNS format if you need it
  • Reminders for unpaid invoices
  • Your session notes, hosted in Luxembourg
  • Your income and expenses, exportable for your tax return

Nothing to run in parallel : Sigmund replaces your spreadsheet and your calendar, it doesn't add to them.

What Sigmund doesn't handle (or not yet)

Sigmund won't bring you new patients. It's not a directory, and it won't become one — if your main need is filling your calendar, other tools do that better.

The patient portal and self-service appointment booking aren't available yet. If that's a deciding factor for you, mention it during the demo.
